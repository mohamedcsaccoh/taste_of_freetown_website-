Welcome to our table

Food with roots.
Flavour without borders.

Taste of Freetown is a modern restaurant that brings together the rich flavours of Sierra Leone and the world. We started with a simple idea: create a place where great food, good people and a warm atmosphere come together.

Our goal is to give you a memorable dining experience with fresh ingredients, authentic recipes and excellent service. Whether you are joining us for a family meal, a celebration or a quick lunch, there is always a seat for you.
